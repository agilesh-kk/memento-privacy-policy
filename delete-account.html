Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your Memento account and associated data.

How to request deletion

1
Send a deletion request

Email Axis Studio from the email address associated with your Memento account. Include your Memento account email and clearly state that you want your account and associated data deleted.

2
Account verification

We may verify that the request belongs to the account owner before processing it.

3
Deletion processing

After verification, we will process the request and delete the account and associated Memento data, subject to legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory retention requirements.

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted?

Where applicable, deletion includes associated account and user-generated data such as:

  • Account/profile information
  • Profile picture and bio
  • Messages and associated conversation data
  • Status updates and associated media
  • Timeline events and memories
  • Watch2gether content associated with your account
  • Other personal information associated with your Memento account

Data that may be retained

If information must be retained for a legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or regulatory purpose, we will retain only what is necessary for that purpose for the required period.

Important

Account deletion is intended to be permanent. Make sure you want to delete your account before submitting the request.
